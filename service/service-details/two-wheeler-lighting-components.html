Two-Wheeler Lighting Components

Aditya Enterprises manufactures precision-moulded two-wheeler lighting components, headlamp housings, turn indicator covers, reflex reflectors, and rear tail lamp assemblies for leading motorcycle and scooter brands including Piaggio, Bajaj Auto, Royal Enfield, and Kawasaki.

Two-wheeler lighting demands compact packaging, lightweight construction, aerodynamic styling, and high vibration resistance. Our verified product range includes the Motard Headlamp Housing (Moterd Housing H/L) for Piaggio, manufactured to exacting OEM dimensional and aesthetic tolerances.

Aerodynamic Styling, Optical Clarity & OEM Reliability

Using multi-cavity precision injection moulding, we produce flawless glossy and textured black housings, snap-fit bezel rings, and optical-grade amber, red, and clear lenses with zero flash and minimal gate vestige.

Our engineering materials include automotive-grade ABS, optical Polycarbonate (PC), PMMA, and impact-modified PP. All components undergo rigorous in-process optical clarity checks, photometric compliance verifications, and ultrasonic hermetic leak testing.

  • Motard Headlamp Housing (H/L) for Piaggio.
  • Precision motorcycle turn indicator lenses.
  • Lightweight, aerodynamic polymer design.
  • High-gloss black & textured surface finishes.
  • Optical clarity & UV non-yellowing resins.
  • Ultrasonic welding for hermetic lens sealing.

Our road safety reflectors (raised pavement markers) are manufactured using uncoloured ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene) natural plastic, delivering up to 50-ton load resistance, high retro-reflective visibility in low-light conditions, and up to 10 years of weather resistance.
