Plastic Injection Moulding & Sub-Assembly

Aditya Enterprises delivers high-precision plastic injection moulding solutions with machine capacities ranging from 150T to 750T. Operating across two specialized manufacturing facilities in PCNTDA, Bhosari, Pune, we serve leading automotive OEMs and industrial manufacturers across India.

Our core capabilities encompass the complete manufacturing lifecycle—from incoming raw material inspection of uncoloured ABS, PA6, PP, and polycarbonates, through precision injection moulding with approved PPS process standards, to sub-assembly, ultrasonic welding, hot plate welding, insert moulding, and final testing.

Why Choose Our Services

With a dual-plant setup in Bhosari MIDC, robust IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 certifications, and a zero-defect quality approach, Aditya Enterprises is your trusted partner for high-tolerance automotive components.

  • Machine capacities from 150T to 750T.
  • Dual plant setup in Sector 10 & Sector 7, Bhosari.
  • IATF 16949, ISO 9001 & ISO 14001 certified.
  • Ultrasonic and hot plate welding capabilities.
  • 100% in-process inspection and traceability.
  • Zero-defect approach & standard operating procedures.

From headlamp housings and tail lamps for two-wheelers and tractors to 50-ton load road safety reflectors, our manufacturing engineering guarantees unmatched durability, dimensional accuracy, and timely delivery.
